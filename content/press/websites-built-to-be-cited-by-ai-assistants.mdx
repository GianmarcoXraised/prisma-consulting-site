Prisma House, a London-based studio that builds custom CRMs, business systems and websites for growing companies, today set out its approach to building websites for AI-driven search. As buyers increasingly ask AI assistants for recommendations, a website now has to be understood and cited by answer engines as well as ranked by search engines.

Prisma House builds every website on five principles: a plain, one-sentence definition of what the company is and does; structured data that names the organisation, its people and its services; pages that answer the real questions buyers ask, in short, quotable answers; fast performance on mobile; and the same facts stated consistently everywhere the company appears online.

"AI assistants don't reward the cleverest copy. They reward the clearest facts, repeated consistently by sources they trust," said Gianmarco Giordaniello, Founder and CEO of Prisma House. "If a machine can't tell in one sentence what you do, it won't recommend you."

Prisma House builds new websites and redesigns existing ones. A focused redesign typically takes six to ten weeks; a full build for a larger company takes three to five months.
