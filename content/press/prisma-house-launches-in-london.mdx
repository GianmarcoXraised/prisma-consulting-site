Prisma House, a London-based studio that builds custom CRMs, business systems and websites for growing companies, today launched its new website, prisma-house.com. The studio designs software around how a company actually works, and starts every project from the marketing strategy rather than from a template.

Prisma House was founded by Gianmarco Giordaniello, Founder and CEO and an Official Member of the Forbes Business Council 2026. He started it after building and running his own media and publishing businesses, along with the CRM, platforms and websites they run on.

"Most growing companies run on software built for someone else's business. They pay per seat for a CRM their team works around, and for a website designed before anyone decided what it should sell," said Gianmarco Giordaniello, Founder and CEO of Prisma House. "We shape the strategy, then we build it, and the client owns every line."

## What Prisma House builds

Custom CRMs and back-office systems, client portals, websites and website redesigns, content management systems, SaaS products, and AI agents that take over repetitive operational work. Examples with real screens are available at [prisma-house.com/work](https://prisma-house.com/work).

## How engagements start

Most projects begin with the Strategy & Scoping Sprint: two weeks, fixed price. Prisma House maps how the business works, defines what to build and why, and delivers a scoped plan with a clear budget, including an honest recommendation when off-the-shelf software is the better choice. Clients own everything that is built: code, data, hosting and documentation.
