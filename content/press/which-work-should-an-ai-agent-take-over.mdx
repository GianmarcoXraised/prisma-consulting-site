Prisma House, a London-based studio that builds custom CRMs, business systems and websites for growing companies, today set out how it decides where AI agents belong in a company's operations.

The approach starts with one question: which chain of repetitive tasks sets the ceiling on how much the business can deliver? In one system Prisma House has built, every new piece of work triggered the same chain: write it up, distribute it, prepare the social posts, chase the reply. Agents now take one input and produce the write-up, the distribution, the posts and the follow-ups. Follow-up messages are drafted and queued for a person to approve; nothing is sent on its own. The system is shown at [prisma-house.com/work/ai-agents](https://prisma-house.com/work/ai-agents).

"The useful question isn't 'where can we use AI?' It's 'which chain of work is stopping us from shipping more?'" said Gianmarco Giordaniello, Founder and CEO of Prisma House. "Automate that chain end to end, keep a human where judgement matters, and the ceiling moves."
