As companies set their 2027 budgets, Prisma House, a London-based studio that builds custom CRMs, business systems and websites for growing companies, is urging leadership teams to scope software and website projects before they fund them.

The Strategy & Scoping Sprint takes two weeks at a fixed price. It delivers a map of the company's processes and sales funnel, a positioning check against its buyers, a scoped build plan with priorities and budget, and an honest recommendation, including when off-the-shelf software is the better call.

"Most software budgets are set before anyone has mapped how the business actually works. That's how companies end up paying for features nobody uses and missing the one system they needed," said Gianmarco Giordaniello, Founder and CEO of Prisma House. "Two weeks of scoping is the cheapest decision in the whole project."
